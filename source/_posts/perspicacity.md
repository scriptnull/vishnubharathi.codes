I learnt this new word: "perspicacity". You might wondering why I am writing a blog post and not a flashcard for it. That is a fair thought! I'll tell you why.

It is because I learnt this word from this small tamil book.

![Bright Future by Vedhathiri Maharishi](/images/perspicacity-book.jpg)

This is exactly the thing I love about books. You never know where it takes you!

Before I tell you what the word means, I want to talk a little bit about the book.

The book is written in a language called Tamil. The title of the book translates to "Bright Future".

The book is a transcript of a speech delivered by Vedhathiri Maharishi. He created Manavalakalai Yoga (simplified yoga) that can be understood by anybody. The slang used in the book is "Thooya Tamil" (pure Tamil). The same Tamil that 2k [?] old poems dated 1600+ years back and at the same time contains English explaining the text explaining the meaning of the word "perspicacity" [?].

Here is the quote from it:

> Perspicacity: Quick insight of the mind linking past experiences, present circumstances and future results taking a right decision in right time and following and getting benefit.

With the advent of AI, I have been thinking about what do I need to navigate this? I have come across some of those words people mention to describe what you need to succeed in AI: "moat", "agency", etc.

Take "agency" for example. Agency means the capacity to act and get things done. I often relate it with ownership. Owning some piece of the system and acting on it to get business value is my understanding of agency.

Now I give [?] (and more) are all needed before AI to go well.

Now with AI especially in software development, I think we need "perspicacity". Here is my theory on why: AI is producing tons of code and documents a.k.a AI slop. Now almost every engineer I talk to is feeling overwhelmed by it. I myself find it too much to digest and I would say that it is my number one factor for doing hard things now.

Of course I went on a search to find answers for how to overcome and adapt. First I was trying to read all the slop - that didn't go well. I tried the "grill-me" skill -

So here is my advice to myself and to you (if you are looking to work better with AI): Do things that increase your perspicacity.

Do what? again?

- **Gain insights**: Your mind needs to get insight - the more & wider insights you have, better it is. e.g. while debugging a system your knowledge of the words p50, p95, p99 is an insight that you can leverage to debug faster and more thoroughly. Now you should read, practice, and internalize things you think will help you generate insights.
- **Get help from time**: Take past experience, present circumstance and potential future results and understand tradeoffs and try to choose a good solution for the problem. If it turned out that it is not a good solution, don't worry much - now it has become a past experience and should help you in future.
- **Act on the insights**. Now this is agency.
- **Prioritize your health**: Both physical and mental! Now this the important part. In order for right insights to strike you at the right time you have to have a peaceful state of mind. Get good sleep, do things that make you happy, relax, meditate, journal, take naps, - these might be some ways to achieve peace.

Now this is what I am going to do: Just ask "what is going to increase my perspicacity?" in a given situation and follow-through.

Oh! and the answer to AI slop that I have found so far is to ask a lot of questions to it or make it ask a lot of questions to you (grill me skill.) If you feel like you are drowning, cut scope or ship in smaller milestones.

That's all I have for now.
