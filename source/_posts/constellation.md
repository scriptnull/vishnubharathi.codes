I came across a beautiful poem written by my sister Ananya recently. She seem to have written it as a stress buster while preparing for her exams.

I found it to be special because of the level of art that was involved in making it. First she wanted the paper to be coffee-ish colored. So she made coffee and dipped white A4 sheets and dried them to get the perfect texture.

I am sure that there is more to "dip and color" process - I will need to ask more about it to her some other time.

After writing on that paper she had rolled it up and used a thread around the roll to tie with shoe lace knots. It felt like the scrolls shown in naruto.
