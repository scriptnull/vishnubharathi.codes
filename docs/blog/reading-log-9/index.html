Reading Log #9

2026-11-11
blogging, books, reading

I originally tried publishing my reading logs every week: logs #1 to #4 came out every week, #5 to #8 came out approx every 2 weeks, now with this reading log I have stepped into 3 weeks. Lol, diminishing effect of novelty, huh? I am going to see if I can get the reading logs out every 10 days. So 3 times a month.

With that said, I didn’t get a chance to read a lot of stuff these 3 weeks. I have been traveling (entire weekend in the woods), celebrating (Happy Diwali to you!), and working a lot. So had to steal from my reading time :)

I also came to this weird realization that, “I can’t read all the books on my shelf and all the blog posts in my reader” and I have to be mindful about what I pick. (I bet on my curiosities to help me here)

Here is what I read from 23rd October to 11th November 2026.
